# GroverLab Research Protocol

GroverLab is intended for educational research contexts such as TOCE/SIGCSE-style
studies.

## Logging Policy

Research logging must be anonymous. Do not log raw dataset contents, names,
emails, IP addresses, or free-text student identifiers.

Approved log fields include aggregate simulation and interaction data such as
dataset size, qubit count, iteration count, noise mode, success flag, and timing.
